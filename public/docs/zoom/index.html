What Ayonix Zoomer does

Ayonix Zoomer is an attendance-integrity console for organisations that deliver compulsory training over Zoom — compliance, certification and regulated-industry courses where the organisation must be able to evidence who actually attended.

Zoom remains the meeting application. Ayonix Zoomer sits alongside it and gives the meeting host a single screen showing, per participant: whether a face is visible, whether that face matches the person enrolled for the course, whether the camera is on, and whether anything needs the host's attention. It produces an end-of-meeting report.

What it does not do

  • It does not record your meeting, its audio, or its video.
  • It does not judge attention, comprehension, mood or any other psychological state. It reports only observable facts such as "a face is visible" or "the camera is off".
  • It does not read your Zoom chat, files, or cloud recordings.
  • It never automatically fails a trainee. Every detection is reviewed by a person.

Requirements

  • A Zoom account that is allowed to install Marketplace apps. If your Zoom administrator has restricted app installation, ask them to pre-approve Ayonix Zoomer.
  • An Ayonix Zoomer account for your organisation. Contact sales@ayonix.com if you do not have one.
  • A modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox).

Adding the app

The app is connected from inside Ayonix Zoomer. You authorise it once, with your Zoom account.

  1. Sign in to https://zoomer.ayonix.com as an administrator.
  2. Open 設定 (Settings) from the left-hand navigation.
  3. In the Zoom連携 (Zoom integration) panel, select Zoomアカウントを接続 (Connect Zoom account).
  4. You are redirected to Zoom. Sign in if prompted, review the permissions being requested, and select Allow.
  5. Zoom returns you to Ayonix Zoomer. The Zoom panel now shows 接続済み (Connected) together with the date of connection.

You can also install from the Zoom App Marketplace listing by selecting Add, which runs the same authorisation flow.

Using the app

1. Enrol your trainees

Go to 受講者登録 (Enrolment) and add trainees individually or by CSV import. For identity verification, capture a reference photo for each trainee with their explicit consent. Only a mathematical face template is stored — the photograph itself is not retained.

2. Create or link a training session

In 研修管理 (Training sessions), create a session. Either select Zoomミーティングを作成 (Create Zoom meeting) to have the app create the meeting for you, or link an existing meeting from your Zoom account. Assign the trainees who should attend.

3. Run the meeting

Host the meeting in Zoom exactly as you normally would. In Ayonix Zoomer, open ライブ会議 (Live Meeting) and select the meeting. The console shows participants with their current status, highlights anyone needing attention, and lists events as they open and resolve.

4. Review and report

After the meeting, レポート (Reports) gives attendance and participation figures per trainee, exportable as CSV, JSON or PDF. イベント (Events) and 証跡・ログ (Audit log) hold the detailed record.

Tell your participants. Notify attendees that identity verification is in use before the session, and obtain the consent your jurisdiction requires. Face data is biometric personal information — in Japan it is 個人識別符号 under the APPI.

Removing the app

Option A — disconnect from within Ayonix Zoomer

  1. Sign in to https://zoomer.ayonix.com as an administrator.
  2. Open 設定 (Settings) → Zoom連携.
  3. Select 切断 (Disconnect).

The stored Zoom access and refresh tokens are deleted immediately and the app stops calling the Zoom API on your behalf.

Option B — uninstall from the Zoom App Marketplace

  1. Sign in to the Zoom App Marketplace.
  2. Select Manage → Added Apps, or search for Ayonix Zoomer.
  3. Select the app, then select Remove.
  4. Confirm the removal.

What happens to your data

  • Zoom credentials — deleted as soon as the app is disconnected or removed.
  • Zoom meeting and participant records already synchronised — retained in your Ayonix Zoomer account, because they form part of your training attendance evidence. They are deleted when you delete your Ayonix Zoomer account.
  • Face templates, monitoring events and evidence images — these are Ayonix Zoomer data, never sent to Zoom. They are deleted according to the retention period you configure, or on request.

To have all of your data deleted, email support@ayonix.com from the account administrator's address. We confirm deletion in writing.

Troubleshooting

"Invalid redirect" when connecting

The redirect address registered for the Zoom app does not match the one being requested. Contact support — this is a configuration issue on our side, not something you can fix.

The Zoom panel still shows "not connected" after authorising

Authorisation expires if it takes longer than 10 minutes. Select Zoomアカウントを接続 again and complete it in one go. If the panel reports a specific error, include that message when contacting support.

"Create Zoom meeting" fails

The connection was authorised before meeting-creation permission was granted. Disconnect and reconnect the Zoom account to refresh the permissions.

Participants are not matched to trainees

Matching uses the email address, then the trainee ID in the display name, then the name. Ask attendees to join with the email registered for the course, or match them manually in the live console.

Ayonix Zoomer とは

Ayonix Zoomer は、Zoomで実施するコンプライアンス研修・資格研修などにおいて、 「誰が実際に受講したか」を証跡として残すための本人確認・受講監視システムです。 会議そのものはZoomで行い、本アプリは主催者向けの管理画面として並行して動作します。

録画は行いません。理解度・集中度・心理状態の判定も行いません。 カメラ映像から観測できる事象（顔が映っているか、登録者と一致するか、カメラがオンか等）のみを扱います。 自動判定のみで受講不可とすることはなく、必ず管理者が確認します。

追加（インストール）方法

  1. https://zoomer.ayonix.com に管理者としてログインします。
  2. 左メニューの「設定」を開きます。
  3. 「Zoom連携」の「Zoomアカウントを接続」を選択します。
  4. Zoomの認可画面で内容を確認し、「許可」を選択します。
  5. Ayonix Zoomer に戻り、「接続済み」と表示されれば完了です。

Zoom App Marketplace のアプリページから Add を選択しても、同じ認可フローが実行されます。

利用方法

  1. 受講者登録：受講者を登録し、本人の同意のうえで本人確認用の顔情報を登録します（原画像は保存しません）。
  2. 研修管理：研修を作成し、Zoomミーティングを作成または既存の会議と紐付け、受講者を割り当てます。
  3. ライブ会議：Zoomで通常どおり会議を開催し、本アプリで受講状況をリアルタイムに確認します。
  4. レポート／イベント／証跡・ログ：終了後に出席状況を集計し、CSV・JSON・PDFで出力できます。

受講者には、本人確認を実施する旨を事前に通知し、必要な同意を取得してください。 顔情報は個人識別符号（個人情報保護法）に該当します。

削除（アンインストール）方法

方法A：アプリ内で切断する

  1. https://zoomer.ayonix.com に管理者としてログインします。
  2. 「設定」→「Zoom連携」を開きます。
  3. 「切断」を選択します。保存されているZoomのトークンは即時削除されます。

方法B：Zoom App Marketplace から削除する

  1. Zoom App Marketplace にサインインします。
  2. Manage → Added Apps で Ayonix Zoomer を選択します。
  3. Remove を選択し、確認します。

削除後、Zoomの認証情報は削除されます。既に同期済みの出席記録等は受講証跡として Ayonix Zoomer 内に保持され、設定した保存期間の経過後、またはご依頼により削除されます。 全データの削除をご希望の場合は support@ayonix.com までご連絡ください。
